# MdxTab

Used inside [MdxTabs](MdxTabs.mdx).

<MdxTabs>
  <MdxTab title="First">Content of first tab</MdxTab>
  <MdxTab title="Second">Content of second tab</MdxTab>
</MdxTabs>

## title

- `title="Tab name"`: <MdxTabs><MdxTab title="Tab name">Content</MdxTab></MdxTabs>

## tabId

- `tabId="my-tab"`:

<MdxTabs activeTabId="tab2">
  <MdxTab tabId="tab1" title="Tab 1">
    Content of tab 1
  </MdxTab>
  <MdxTab tabId="tab2" title="Tab 2">
    Content of tab 2
  </MdxTab>
</MdxTabs>

## subtitle

- `subtitle="hint text"`:

<MdxTabs>
  <MdxTab title="Tab" subtitle="hint text">
    Content
  </MdxTab>
</MdxTabs>

## icon

- `icon="stars"`:

<MdxTabs>
  <MdxTab title="Tab" icon="stars">
    Content
  </MdxTab>
</MdxTabs>

## style

- `style="normal"`:

<MdxTabs>
  <MdxTab title="normal" style="normal">
    Content
  </MdxTab>
</MdxTabs>

- `style="uppercase"`:

<MdxTabs>
  <MdxTab title="uppercase" style="uppercase">
    Content
  </MdxTab>
</MdxTabs>

## active

- `active={true}`:

<MdxTabs>
  <MdxTab title="Tab 1">Content 1</MdxTab>
  <MdxTab title="Tab 2" active={true}>
    Content 2
  </MdxTab>
</MdxTabs>

## canClose

- `canClose={true}`:

<MdxTabs>
  <MdxTab title="Closable" canClose={true}>
    Content
  </MdxTab>
</MdxTabs>
