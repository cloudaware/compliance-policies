# Remediation

1. Confirm with the workload owner and FinOps team that the Savings Plan
   expiration was intentional.
2. In the Azure portal, open **Cost Management + Billing** and select
   **Savings plans** to review the expired plan, eligible usage, and cost impact.
3. Compare current eligible compute demand, the intended scope, term, and
   hourly commitment with the available pricing options.
4. If replacement coverage is justified, purchase a new Savings Plan with the
   approved scope and commitment. Azure Savings Plans are not renewed in place.
5. Configure advance-expiry monitoring and assign an owner so the commitment
   can be reviewed before its next expiry date.
