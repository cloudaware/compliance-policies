# Remediation

Review reservations that are approaching expiration and determine whether to renew, purchase an alternative reservation, or allow the reservation to expire. Taking timely action helps preserve cost savings and prevents unexpected billing increases.

## Using the Azure Portal

1. Review Usage and Recommendations
    - Navigate to the reservation's overview to evaluate its utilization over the commitment term.
    - Review Azure’s recommendations, which may include renewing the reservation, adjusting the quantity, or selecting a different scope.
    - Assess whether the reservation has delivered cost benefits and if your current or projected usage patterns justify a new commitment.

2. Take Action
    - If continued coverage is required, purchase a new reservation with the same or modified terms.
    - If the reservation is no longer needed (e.g., the associated resources have been decommissioned), take no action. The reservation will expire automatically, and any remaining resources will be billed at pay-as-you-go rates.
    - If requirements have changed, acquire a reservation with a different scope, term, or resource type that better aligns with your organization’s needs.
